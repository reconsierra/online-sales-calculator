# online-sales-calculator

Here's what's this tool achieves:

Three sliders — 
-sale price, 
-shipping, 
-pieces sold per month

Slider adjustment:
-select your required average (or specific) sell price
-select your standard shipping price
-select your sales qty per month

Tick the checkbox to switch into monthly sales/cost view, which shows turnover, per-sale fees, subscription cost, total costs, and net payout side by side (Unticked stays in the simple per-sale view)

Table sorts by cheapest first (either lowest per-sale fee or lowest monthly total cost depending on mode), with the bar chart updating to match
